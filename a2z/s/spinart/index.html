Spin Art Turntable

Make a machine that spins a piece of paper--then squeeze on paint.

Add a motor cable
Flip the whole thing upside down, and place on a square board.
Tape it to the board.
Turn rightside up, and you're ready to plug it in to a Cricket or LEGO RCX, and then program the motor.

Use a garbage bag or newspapers to protect the floor. Fill a plastic squeeze bottle with washable (tempera) paint.

Tape a piece of paper to the turntable. Turn it on, then gently squeeze paint on the spinning paper.

Try to make a spiral shape by starting in the center and moving your arm straight towards you (while gently squeezing the paint bottle).

Experiment with different paints and markers.

You can try squeezing tempera paint from a bottle...

Or you can put some fingerpaint on your paper, and make patterns with your fingers...
Or try watercolor paint and a brush...
Experiment, and display the colorful results!
