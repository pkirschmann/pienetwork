Drawing Cup Machine
Keith Braaafladt from Science Museum of Minnesota writes, "I first heard about this idea when Margaret Pezalla-Granlund and I taught a 'Spin Art' workshop at the Fort Worth Mindfest event. Diane Willow described a little invention that balanced on three marker legs. (For the Spin Art workshop I made one using clothespins holding onto paint-soaked sponge feet.) This drawing machine uses LEGO Mindstorms RCX brick to control the shimmy-shake movement." ~
