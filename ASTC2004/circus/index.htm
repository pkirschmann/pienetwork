"PIE Three-Ring Circus" workshop

In this workshop we used the Crickets to explore three themes: movement, light, and sound.

Warm-Up Activity:
Social Crickets

To get started, we tried out Crickets that were pre-programmed to chirp. The Crickets communicated with each other by sending infrared signals, and harmonized the sound of their chirps.
